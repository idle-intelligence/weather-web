about

The estimate is a distance-weighted average of the nearest stations within 100 km, corrected for the target point's elevation, with dew point averaged as vapour pressure, wind averaged as vector components, and pressure kept at sea level like the stations report it.
Everything runs in your browser: station selection, weighting and corrections are the weather Rust library compiled to WebAssembly; only fetching the station list and the live observations happens in JavaScript.
