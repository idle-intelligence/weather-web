about

The estimate is a distance-weighted average of the nearest stations within 100 km, corrected for the target point's elevation, for reducing sea-level pressure to that elevation, and for wind averaged as vector components rather than speed and direction separately.
Everything runs in your browser: station selection, weighting and corrections are the weather Rust library compiled to WebAssembly; only fetching the station list and the live observations happens in JavaScript.
